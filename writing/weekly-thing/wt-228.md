### [Nifty Chess](https://niftychess.com)

Cool app that let's you mint your chess game into an NFT. This original version uses [Gnosis](https://www.gnosischain.com) and supports [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com) and [lichess.org](https://lichess.org), and the new version [Treasure Chess](https://treasure.chess.com) uses [Polygon](https://polygon.technology) and only Chess.com. I think it is a neat idea to be able to capture a game as a unique NFT. Similar to a [POAP](https://poap.fun) capturing an event.
