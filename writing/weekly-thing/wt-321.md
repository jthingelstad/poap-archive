## Issue

My mind also got on fire with POAPs this week. More than half of you just rolled your eyes. 🙄

When I first found POAPs the idea of claiming a POAP for a location was something I wanted right away. Well they have made that a reality and location drops are now a feature. I created [my very first location POAP](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/05/25/i-created-my-very-first.html) and even [recorded a video claiming it](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/05/25/screen-recording-claiming-my-very.html). Awesome. So, I wanted to make something with this. 🤔

**Thus the [612 POAP Challenge](https://www.thingelstad.com/projects/612-poap-challenge/?ref=weekly-thing-321) was created!** 😲

I love that I was able to create an entire activity on the POAP platform. 🤩

## Journal: I created my very first location-based POAP. Our “Peanuts Tree” is a popular spo...

[May 25, 2025 at 7:42 AM](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/05/25/i-created-my-very-first.html)

I created my very first **location-based** [POAP](https://poap.xyz). Our “[Peanuts Tree](https://www.thingelstad.com/magic-pines/charlie-brown-tree/)” is a popular spot on Cannon Lake and you can now [mint a POAP](https://poap.gallery/drops/190337) if you visit it. I’m going to place a QR code by it so visitors can get more info. Will anybody do it? I don’t know, but it is fun either way. See [screen recording of mint](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/05/25/screen-recording-claiming-my-very.html).
