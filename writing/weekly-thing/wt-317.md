### [Minnebar 19 POAP](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/04/28/minnebar-poap.html)
Apr 28, 2025 at 9:02 PM

[Minnebar](https://minnestar.org/minnebar/) 19 is coming up this Saturday and I couldn’t pass up the opportunity to create a special [Minnebar 19 POAP token](https://poap.gallery/drops/189532) for folks at the event to get and commemorate the event! I did a [POAP for Minnebar 17](https://www.thingelstad.com/2023/03/04/minnebar-poaps.html) two years ago.

For this [POAP](https://poap.xyz) I reached out to Meg Steuer at Minnestar and she was kind enough to share the graphics that are being used for the t-shirts. It was a super cool shirt and after consider it for a bit it worked absolutely perfect for an animated image. I'm loving this loop!

![](https://cdn.uploads.micro.blog/890/2025/minnebar-19-poap.gif)

I have several hundred tokens loaded on three different [POAP NFC discs](https://iyk.app/shop/disc) that will be placed around the event. Just bring your phone near the disc and tap the center -- you’ll get directed to a link to mint your token!

If you want to get ready for the day, download the **POAP Home** app to be ready for [iOS](https://poap.xyz/ios) and [Android](https://poap.xyz/android). Using this app you can also upload photos from the event to POAP Memories to share with others that hold the same token.

I’ll also be sharing my [You Met Me](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/04/28/we-met-at-minnebar-poap.html) POAP as well as a one for my [How to Newsletter Session](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/04/28/minnebar-how-to-newsletter-session.html).
