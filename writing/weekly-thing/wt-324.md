### [Stone Arch Bridge Fest](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/06/14/stone-arch-bridge-fest.html)
Jun 14, 2025 at 6:00 PM

Today was Mazie’s birthday and one of the things she decided we should do is the [Stone Arch Bridge Festival](https://stonearchbridgefestival.com). It had been a very long time since we had been to this and I was really impressed by the size of it. There were so many artists and vendors. It felt to me like it had a particular Minnesota flare to it, with a lot of art that was locally inspired. We spent about four hours but could have spent the entire day there.

![](https://files.thingelstad.com/weekly-thing/324/journal/b723b9f0df.jpg)

Some artists that caught our eye:

- [Robots in Rowboats](https://www.robotsinrowboats.com)
- [A.D. Hogan](https://adhoganart.com)
- [Bill Nagel](https://bill-nagel.com)
- [Skape Designs](https://www.skapedesigns.com)
- [James Powell Art](https://www.jamespowellart.com)
- [CrewlArt](https://crewlart.com)
- [Eric Beauchamp](https://beaubois.us)
- [H. Ward Miles](https://www.hwardmiles.com)

Mazie bought a piece of art with her own money -- her very first art purchase from [H. Ward Miles](https://www.hwardmiles.com)

![](https://files.thingelstad.com/weekly-thing/324/journal/2b6b2e3849.jpg)

I also really liked these very local artist creating location pieces. Maybe I could get one of them to participate in a future year of the [612 POAP Challenge](https://www.thingelstad.com/projects/612-poap-challenge/). 😊

- [Little Dipper Art](https://www.littledipper.store)
- [Set Adrift Art](https://setadriftart.com)
- [Minnesota Awesome](https://minnesota-awesome.com)

We grabbed some switchel from [Hobby Farmer](https://www.hobbyfarmerfoods.com) on our way heading back home!
