### [PhizFans Multisig Experiment](https://www.reddit.com/r/ethfinance/comments/vfnoiz/daily_general_discussion_june_19_2022/icynizu/)

I so adore this experiment! [Superphiz](https://superphiz.eth.xyz) gave a [Phiz Fans POAP](https://poap.gallery/event/10348) out. He then took anyone that got that POAP and had an ENS name setup (I suspect to prove some level of familiarity) and made those 52 holders the joint owners of the [Phiz Fans](https://gnosis-safe.io/app/eth:0x54EBEb54a5D1942073e28589F27e165953EE0b46/home) wallet! He then transferred an NFT into it. The wallet requires 6 holders to approve any actions. Now they have transferred their own ENS name into it, as well as other assets. Wild! 🤠
