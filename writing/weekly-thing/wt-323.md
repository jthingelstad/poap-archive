## Issue

Folks have been starting to play the **[612 POAP Challenge](https://www.thingelstad.com/projects/612-poap-challenge/)**! I got our [family out collecting](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/06/14/got-a-recursive-selfie-while.html) as well. I love that I could create this whole game on [POAP](https://poap.xyz)! Come and [join the fun](https://buttondown.com/612-poap-challenge). Read more on [612 POAP Challenge](https://www.thingelstad.com/projects/612-poap-challenge/) as well. I love the vibe of this event so of course I had to register `poapchallenge.com`. 🤩
