Basilica of Saint Mary — 612 POAP Challenge

From its ornate domes to its towering columns, the Basilica is a spiritual and structural masterpiece. Architecture Aficionados will appreciate the harmony of faith and form in this sacred space. This POAP marks your visit to one of Minneapoliss most iconic works of design in the 612 POAP Challenge.
