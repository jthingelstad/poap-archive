Martin Olav Sabo Bridge — 612 POAP Challenge

Dedicated to a beloved public servant and designed for cyclists and pedestrians, the Martin Olav Sabo Bridge proves that even infrastructure can be people-first. Its tall white pylon and cable-stayed design are impossible to miss as it soars over busy Hiawatha Avenue. It's a bold statement for alternative transportation and urban design. This Bridge Builder POAP celebrates your pedestrian-powered path through the 612 POAP Challenge.
