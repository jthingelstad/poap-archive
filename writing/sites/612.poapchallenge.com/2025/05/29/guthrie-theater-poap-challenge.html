Guthrie Theater — 612 POAP Challenge

With its glowing blue box and cantilevered 'endless bridge,' the Guthrie is where architecture and storytelling collide. Inside, world-class performances unfold on its three stages, while outside, it frames the river like a cinematic set. Whether you came for the view or the drama, you experienced a cornerstone of Minneapolis culture. This Culture Collector POAP marks your stop at a theater that dares to think big in the 612 POAP Challenge.
