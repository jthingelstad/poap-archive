How to Play

Explore Minneapolis. Collect digital art. Discover the city like never before.

The 612 POAP Challenge is a citywide scavenger hunt running through Labor Day – Monday, September 1, 2025. Across Minneapolis, 31 iconic locations await – each tied to a unique POAP (short for Proof of Attendance Protocol, pronounced “poh-ap”), a digital token you can collect as a memento of your visit. It’s like geocaching, but with art, technology, and hometown pride.

Whether you’re a lifelong local or new to the Twin Cities, this is your invitation to experience Minneapolis in a fresh, fun, and meaningful way.

📨 Make sure to signup to get updates on the 612 POAP Challenge!



📍 What’s the Challenge?

Visit any of the 31 featured locations and use the POAP Home app to claim a beautifully illustrated token created in the spirit of the 612 Series by Erik Halaas and StayNftyMpls. From local legends to architectural gems, these POAPs highlight what makes this city great.

See map of all locations. 🗺️

✨ Bonus twist: There are also two hidden “easter egg” locations not listed anywhere – they’re out there if you’re curious enough to find them!

🧠 Complete Categories & Earn More

Locations are grouped into five themed categories, each celebrating a different facet of Minneapolis. Complete a full category and you’ll unlock a Category Collector POAP – a special token to mark your achievement.

🎯 Go the distance and collect all 31 official location POAPs (easter eggs not required), and you’ll earn the rare and coveted 612 Completionist POAP – the ultimate badge of honor.

🪙 What’s a POAP?

POAP stands for Proof of Attendance Protocol – a digital collectible that proves you were somewhere special. Think of it like a digital badge or sticker you earn for showing up.

Each POAP in this challenge is:

  • Free to collect
  • Beautifully designed
  • Easy to claim with your phone using POAP Home

It’s your personal souvenir, stored online forever.

📸 Capture the Moment!

When you visit a location, take a selfie or photo and share it via POAP moments! It’s a great way to:

  • Share your journey with others
  • Create a personal visual journal of the challenge
  • Add your story to each place you visit

You can upload your photo right inside the POAP app after claiming your token. These POAP moments can only be created once you have claimed the token.

🧭 How to Join (It’s like Geocaching, but Digital!)

  1. Download the POAP Home App!

  2. Go to a Location: Use the map or list below to find the featured locations

  3. Claim the POAP On-Site: Open the app and check for nearby drops – each token can only be claimed while physically present at the location. You need to be within 100 meters of the specific location to get it! Watch this video if you want to see what happens.

  4. Add a Photo Memory: Snap a pic of yourself or the spot and create a POAP moment

  5. Collect & Explore: Rack up POAPs, finish categories, hunt down easter eggs – it’s up to you!
